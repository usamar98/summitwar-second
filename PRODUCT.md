product

## Users and purpose
Indie founders enter their startups in eight-app community tournaments. Visitors browse the contenders and cast final votes without voter sign-in. Founders and advertisers use protected accounts to manage entries and purchases. Organizers review entries, payments, suspicious votes and promotion fulfillment.

## Brand and principles
Competitive, direct and welcoming. The summit and shield identity comes from the existing app. Keep community winners distinct from paid promotion. Never fabricate entrants, traffic, winners or fulfilled purchases. Give users exact prices and deadlines before payment. Keep the existing dark green visual language and eight entrant colors.

Tournament entry and pre-entry are permanently free. All six sponsor placements cost $100 each. Any sponsor can replace a current placement after verified payment; the current ad stays visible during checkout. Show this takeover policy before purchase, including that being overtaken is not refundable. Preserve existing booking price records.

## Accessibility
Keep keyboard access, visible focus, native controls, readable contrast, honest empty states, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support already present in the project.

## Tournament rewards
Show the rewards comparison directly below the tournament and before the participant list. Every participant receives tournament exposure, community tools, battle cards and a private performance report. Only startups with an actual recorded championship receive a permanent standalone startup page with a website link. Participant links lead to battles or tournament results. Social posts, pinning and interviews require organizer action.
