# SummitWar

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Indie founders enter their startups in eight-app community tournaments. Visitors browse the contenders and cast guest votes without email sign-in. Each network gets one guest vote per match; additional voters on shared Wi-Fi verify with GitHub. Founders and advertisers use protected accounts to manage entries and purchases. Organizers review entries, payments, suspicious votes and promotion fulfillment.

## Brand and principles
Competitive, direct and welcoming. The summit and shield identity comes from the existing app. Keep community winners distinct from paid promotion. Never fabricate entrants, traffic, winners or fulfilled purchases. Give users exact prices and deadlines before payment. Keep the existing dark green visual language and eight entrant colors.

Tournament entry and pre-entry are permanently free. Sponsorship uses fixed one-time prices per card: $100 through all quarterfinal results, $200 through all semifinal results, or $300 for the whole tournament until the next tournament starts, including the final and winner spotlight. Nobody can replace a paid sponsor during its purchased period. Shorter plans release only after all results in that round are finalized, even when processing is delayed. Buyers can then book released cards for a remaining period. One buyer can select any or all six available cards. Preserve immutable per-card prices and the purchased protection plan; publish only after verified payment.

## Accessibility
Keep keyboard access, visible focus, native controls, readable contrast, honest empty states, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support already present in the project.

## Interactive demo
The `/demo` route uses clearly labelled fictional startups and sample votes. Visitors preview protected $100, $200 and $300 sponsors, advance quarterfinal and semifinal results to release shorter plans, and reset every card for the next tournament. Released cards can receive another local sample placement. No demo interaction calls payment APIs or writes Supabase data. Winner pages, badges, reports and social posts remain visual examples; no social post is sent.

## Tournament rewards
Show the rewards comparison directly below the tournament and before the participant list. Every participant receives tournament exposure, community tools, battle cards and a private performance report. Only startups with an actual recorded championship receive a permanent standalone startup page with a do-follow website link. Explicitly name that link in winner rewards. Participant links lead to battles or tournament results. Social posts, pinning and interviews require organizer action.
