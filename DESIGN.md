# SummitWar visual system

## Existing direction
Dark forest surfaces, muted green borders, warm off-white text and a pale lime action color. Geist/Segoe UI sans-serif carries both interface text and headings. Shield/crossed-swords branding and summit motifs are existing assets.

## Homepage hierarchy
A centered community winner is the primary hero focal point. Three clearly labelled paid promotion placements sit on either side. The full tournament bracket follows as the second section. Paid cards retain visible placement prices in empty states and Promoted disclosure after purchase. The center never becomes a paid placement.

## Components and behavior
Use the existing button, field, panel and link vocabulary. Empty sponsor cards use transparent surfaces, fine dashed white outlines and the centered label Sponsor by. Clicking a card opens a centered details dialog for that placement without navigating; the dedicated promotion page still supports multiple-card purchases. On small screens show the winner first, then labelled promotion placements, then the competition. Use restrained full borders rather than decorative side stripes. Keep native focus and reduced-motion behavior.

## Tournament rewards
Show the rewards comparison directly below the tournament and before the participant list. Every participant receives tournament exposure, community tools, battle cards and a private performance report. Only startups with an actual recorded championship receive a permanent standalone startup page with a do-follow website link. Participant links lead to battles or tournament results. Social posts, pinning and interviews require organizer action.

Sponsor booking shows the current per-card paid amount, the minimum bid and a native currency input for a custom higher bid. Empty cards open at $100; occupied cards require at least $1 more than the current amount. Keep a clear combined total for multiple-card purchases and explain the reset before each new tournament.

The demo retains the production hero and bracket layout. A labelled demo control strip offers local takeover and next-tournament previews. Below the rewards comparison, show a sample permanent winner page beside its badge, private report and sample social post, followed by an example sponsor placement with its included benefits. Keep sample and organizer-provided content explicit.
