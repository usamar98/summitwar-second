# SummitWar visual system

## Existing direction
Dark forest surfaces, muted green borders, warm off-white text and a pale lime action color. Geist/Segoe UI sans-serif carries both interface text and headings. Shield/crossed-swords branding and summit motifs are existing assets.

## Homepage hierarchy
A centered community winner is the primary hero focal point. Three clearly labelled paid promotion placements sit on either side. The full tournament bracket follows as the second section. Paid cards retain visible placement prices in empty states and Promoted disclosure after purchase. The center never becomes a paid placement.

## Components and behavior
Use the existing button, field, panel and link vocabulary. Keep purchase selection inline on a dedicated page. On small screens show the winner first, then labelled promotion placements, then the competition. Use restrained full borders rather than decorative side stripes. Keep native focus and reduced-motion behavior.
